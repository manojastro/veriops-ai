# INC-2026-009: checkout CPU saturation during flash sale

Traffic rose 3.5x during a flash sale. CPU reached 94% on all pods while the orders-db pool stayed
around 55%. Scaling from 3 to 6 replicas restored latency. Scaling was safe because the database had
connection headroom.
